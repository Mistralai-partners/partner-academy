# A1: Delegate and review · TASKS

- **Objective:** delegate a multi-step research task to Vibe Work using describe, gather, act, review, and review the result against an acceptance rubric.

- **Scenario (why this matters on the job):** you are handed vague asks constantly. The skill that separates a good delegation from a bad one is describing an outcome and then judging the result, not accepting the first confident answer.

- **Prerequisites:** signed in at chat.mistral.ai/work; a new conversation open. Starter pack: `starter/topic-card.md`, `starter/delegation-prompt-skeleton.md`, `starter/acceptance-rubric.md`.

- **Done when:** all 7 rows of `starter/acceptance-rubric.md` are Yes.

## Steps (in-app)

- Open a new conversation.
- Read `starter/topic-card.md` and fill every TODO in `starter/delegation-prompt-skeleton.md`: outcome, required structure, sources, audience, constraints (length + tone + sourcing rule).
- Paste the completed delegation into the conversation. Do not paste a one-line question.
- Open the Todos panel and watch Vibe Work gather and act. Note what it actually does at each step.
   - *Hint:* if it jumps straight to an answer with no gather steps, your prompt was too thin; add the missing structure or sources and resend.
- Read the result. Check that each claim carries a visible source.
- Refine once in the same thread using the follow-up in the skeleton ("tighten... drop anything you cannot attribute"). Do not start a new conversation.
- Score your output against `starter/acceptance-rubric.md`.

## Acceptance

Pass when every rubric row is Yes: structure present, attribution on every claim, unattributable items flagged, length and tone honored, delegated (not searched), refined in-thread. See `VERIFY.md`.

## Stretch

Ask Vibe Work to list the questions it could not answer from public sources. Those gaps are exactly what a Library would fill (covered in the self-paced VIBEWORK-200).
