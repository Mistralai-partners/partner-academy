# A1: Topic Card

You are a market analyst. Your manager drops this in chat on Monday:

> "Get me a first read on **enterprise adoption of AI coding assistants** before Thursday."

That is the whole ask. No structure, no sources, no length. Your job is to turn this vague ask into a delegated *outcome* for Vibe, then judge whether the result is trustworthy enough to forward.

## Your assigned topic

**Enterprise adoption of AI coding assistants** (last 12 months).

> Facilitator: you may swap in any current, public topic. Keep it researchable from public sources so A1 stays self-contained with no Library or connector dependency.

## The audience

Your manager forwards trusted first reads to a VP. Assume a busy executive reader who wants the signal, the players, and the risks, with every claim traceable.

## Constraints you must impose (you decide the exact values, then hold the output to them)

- **Length:** about one page (roughly 5 to 7 key points).
- **Tone:** neutral, executive.
- **Sourcing:** every claim must carry a visible source; anything unattributable is flagged, not stated as fact.

Use `delegation-prompt-skeleton.md` to turn this card into your delegation, then score the result with `acceptance-rubric.md`.
