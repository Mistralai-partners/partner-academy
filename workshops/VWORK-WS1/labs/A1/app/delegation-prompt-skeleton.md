# A1: Delegation Prompt Skeleton

Fill every TODO, then paste the whole thing into a new Vibe conversation. The point is to delegate an *outcome*, not to type a one-line question. Do not delete the labels; they are what makes Vibe gather and act deliberately.

```
I am preparing a first read on <TODO: topic from topic-card.md>.

Outcome I want:
<TODO: one sentence describing the finished artifact, e.g. "a one-page brief a VP can skim in two minutes">

Required structure:
<TODO: the exact sections, e.g. "1) the most important developments in the last 12 months,
2) the main players, 3) two risks a leader should care about">

Sources to draw on:
<TODO: e.g. "current, public sources only; show where each point comes from">

Audience:
<TODO: e.g. "a busy executive who wants signal, not background">

Constraints:
<TODO: length + tone, e.g. "about one page, 5 to 7 points, neutral executive tone">
<TODO: sourcing rule, e.g. "every claim must carry a visible source; flag anything you cannot attribute rather than stating it as fact">
```

## Refine once (do not re-prompt from scratch)

After the first result, refine in the *same thread*:

```
Tighten this to the points that matter most to my audience. Drop anything you
cannot attribute to a source.
```

## Self-contained note

A1 uses only public sources and a single conversation. No Library, connector, Skill, or prior lab is required.
