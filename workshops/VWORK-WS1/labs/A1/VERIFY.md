# A1: Delegate and review · VERIFY

Self-checkable acceptance. No instructor required.

## Pass condition

Your output satisfies **all 7 rows** of `starter/acceptance-rubric.md`:

- Required structure present.
- Visible source on every factual claim.
- Unattributable claims flagged, not asserted.
- Length honored (about one page / your stated point count).
- Tone honored (neutral, executive).
- Delegated as an outcome (structure + sources + audience + constraints), not a one-line question.
- Improved with one in-thread follow-up, not a fresh re-prompt.

## How to check

- Read each claim and confirm it has a source. If any claim has none, you have not passed row 2. Ask, in the same thread, to "drop anything you cannot attribute," then re-score.
- Confirm your delegation matched the skeleton (all labels filled). A one-line prompt fails row 6 even if the answer looks good.
- Compare shape (not wording) against `solution/reference-output.md`; compare scoring against `solution/rubric-filled.md`.

## Why this is the right check

- The evidence is that you can judge a delegated result, not that you produced a nice paragraph.
- The rubric forces the judgment: an unattributed claim is a draft, not an answer.
- Passing means you can safely forward a first read produced by delegation.
